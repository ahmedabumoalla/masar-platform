2. Regional outlines and representative governorate points (ADM2)
Source: geoBoundaries / OpenStreetMap contributors
Boundary ID: SAU-ADM2-3984122; represented year: 2021
License reported by geoBoundaries: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/
Metadata and original download links:
https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/SAU/ADM2/
Pinned raw source:
https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/tree/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/SAU/ADM2

Adaptations: Arabic labels, regional assignment, county union by region,
24-degree standard-parallel equirectangular projection, subpixel simplification,
interior representative points. Regional outlines are dissolved from ADM2
instead of mixing incompatible ADM1/ADM2 vintages (notably Al Birk, Sharurah
and Farat Ghamid Az Zinad). The separately derived ADM1 silhouette only fills
background coverage gaps. The Ahsa point represents its oasis rather than
the desert polygon centre. Points are not real farms, wells or sensors.
The adapted national background layer remains offered under ODbL 1.0;
the separate adapted ADM2 outlines/points remain offered under CC BY-SA 2.0.
Download the adapted geographic database: /data/saudi-geography.json

3. Supplementary locations
Dammam (regional capital), Al Bayda, Al Muwayh, Al Amwah and Abanat were
missing from the ADM2 input and added at approximate administrative-seat
locations. These additions do not amend the older boundary geometries.

4. Agricultural information
Separately authored editorial summaries, linked to MEWA, SPA, RCU and other
named primary publications inside each profile. No statistical dominance or
live rainfall/groundwater measurements are claimed. Regional evidence is
labelled as regional; it is not imputed to individual governorates.
